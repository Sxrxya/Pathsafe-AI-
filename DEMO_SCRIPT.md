# Chennai Demo Script

1. Open PATHSAFE AI.
2. Keep **Flood scenario** selected. A visible `SIMULATION — NOT LIVE EMERGENCY DATA` marker must remain on screen.
3. Use the pre-filled Chennai destination or edit it for the presentation narrative.
4. Click **Analyze routes**.
5. Compare the three alternatives using time, estimated risk and confidence.
6. Open Research or Data Sources to explain the architecture and provenance.
7. Emphasize that the route risk is a research heuristic and not a validated safety probability.
